Six design principles

Accountable by construction.

These principles shape the data model, workflows, access decisions and evidence LoanOS creates.

01

India is a hard boundary

INR, India-resident borrowers, India storage expectations and India-specific lending controls define the product scope.

02

The regulated entity remains accountable

Partners can operate inside scoped workflows; responsibility for the product, decision, fund flow and customer does not disappear.

03

People remain in command

Material model use stays inventoried, validated, reviewable, explainable and subject to an enforceable kill switch.

04

Evidence follows the action

Policy, consent, reason, actor, document, model, override and communication context is captured during the workflow.

05

Access stays intentional

Tenant, user, role, queue and service boundaries limit who can see or do what, with review and break-glass evidence.

06

Exit is a product capability

Portability, reproducible exports, offboarding and data disposition are part of the tenant lifecycle.

Human-governed AI

Assist the work. Never obscure responsibility.

LoanOS treats AI/model risk as a runtime concern linked to customer-impacting use. It goes beyond a static model register.

Register

Owner, version, use case, materiality, risk tier and permitted purpose are explicit.

Validate

Status, review evidence, monitoring plan and policy requirements determine availability.

Guard

Every proposed call checks switch, model state, purpose and borrower-impact rules.

Review

Material credit impact can route to an accountable human with case context and reasons.

Stop & learn

Trust questions

Ask us the difficult things first.

Does LoanOS claim that using the platform makes a lender compliant?

No. The platform implements and evidences many control patterns, but compliance depends on the regulated entity’s products, policies, people, partners, deployment, integrations and ongoing legal and supervisory interpretation.

Where is primary platform data intended to be stored?

LoanOS is designed around India-hosted primary systems and India-specific storage boundaries. Actual production residency must be confirmed for the selected hosting, backup, observability and third-party integration architecture.

Can an LSP or fintech control the movement of loan funds?

The platform design treats unauthorised partner pass-through control as a hard failure. Permitted flows and exceptions must be explicitly configured and reviewed against the applicable product and regulatory model.

Can AI approve or decline a loan by itself?

The platform can support rule/model assistance, but material credit-impacting use is governed by registered purpose, model state, validation, policy and human-review requirements. The regulated entity defines the accountable decision policy.

Can an institution leave with its data?

The platform includes tenant portability export and evidenced offboarding foundations so records and audit evidence can be returned in a reproducible form. Production exit procedures must be agreed contractually and operationally.
