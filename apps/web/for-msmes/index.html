Customer guidance

Ask for the right information at the right time.

Requirements vary by lender and product. A clear journey explains each request and avoids collecting the same information repeatedly.

Your business identity

Legal structure, registrations where applicable, principal place of business and the people who own or control it.

Your purpose and requested amount

What the finance will enable, how much you need, when it is needed and how the investment supports repayment.

Your recent business activity

Banking and sales evidence, returns or financial statements where applicable, existing obligations and seasonal patterns.

Your consent and verification choices

A clear notice should explain what information is used, for which purpose, who receives it and how to ask for correction or support.
