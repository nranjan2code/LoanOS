The Invoice discounting journey

Finance the receivable with the buyer and settlement in view.

The journey follows a verified invoice, not a generic cash request.

  1. 01

    Submit the receivable

    Capture the invoice, buyer, due date, underlying supply and requested finance.

  2. 02

    Verify the transaction

    Check buyer eligibility, invoice evidence, concentration, limit availability and assignment terms.

  3. 03

    Approve the draw

    Apply programme rules, price the draw, issue documents and record customer acceptance.

  4. 04

    Release against the invoice

    Fund the approved receivable and maintain the link to buyer, due date and settlement instructions.

  5. 05

    Settle and reconcile

    Allocate buyer payment, manage disputes or exceptions, refresh limits and close the draw.

What stays connected
