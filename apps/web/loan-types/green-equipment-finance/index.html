The Green equipment finance journey

Keep the green purpose visible from proposal to outcome.

The journey combines ordinary credit discipline with eligible asset, supplier and programme evidence.

  1. 01

    Define the green upgrade

    Capture the solar, EV, efficient machinery or cleaner-production asset and its expected use.

  2. 02

    Test eligibility and value

    Review supplier, asset eligibility, savings case, borrower capacity and programme conditions.

  3. 03

    Approve the purpose-linked loan

    Set terms, documents, conditions, incentives and reporting obligations under lender policy.

  4. 04

    Release with asset evidence

    Control payment, installation or delivery proof, insurance and approved-purpose evidence.

  5. 05

    Monitor the facility

    Track repayments, asset status, programme reporting, support, collections and closure.

What stays connected

The product context is not split across disconnected handoffs.

Teams work from the same customer and product thread, with access and responsibility shaped by their role.

Policy and decision

People and counterparties

Evidence through closure

Key facts, documents, payment actions, servicing events, support, exceptions and closure remain traceable.
