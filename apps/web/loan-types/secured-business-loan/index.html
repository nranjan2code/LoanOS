The Secured business loan journey

Connect the business case with the security story.

A secured business loan has two linked decisions: the customer can repay, and the lender can create and maintain eligible security.

  1. 01

    Frame purpose and security

    Capture the business need, proposed collateral, ownership, value and repayment capacity.

  2. 02

    Verify the collateral

    Coordinate valuation, legal review, title, insurance and product-specific security conditions.

  3. 03

    Approve the combined case

    Bring business assessment, collateral findings, pricing, authority and key facts together.

  4. 04

    Create and release security

    Complete documentation, charge creation, disbursement gates and evidence of the release.

  5. 05

    Monitor to satisfaction

    Track repayments, insurance, covenants, security changes, collections and final satisfaction.

What stays connected

Policy and decision

People and counterparties

Evidence through closure
