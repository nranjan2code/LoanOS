The MSME term loan journey

Turn a growth plan into a controlled term-loan journey.

The journey links the proposed investment to capacity, cash flow, release milestones and the long-term account story.

  1. 01

    Define the investment

    Capture the expansion plan, use of funds, promoters, expected benefit and required amount.

  2. 02

    Assess viability

    Review business cash flow, project economics, repayment capacity, policy and approvals.

  3. 03

    Sanction the structure

    Set the tenor, repayment profile, conditions, key facts and execution requirements.

  4. 04

    Release against milestones

    Track approved conditions, supplier or borrower payment, asset evidence and disbursement controls.

  5. 05

    Serve the term loan

    Manage schedule, statements, changes, monitoring, collections and closure.

What stays connected

The product context is not split across disconnected handoffs.

Teams work from the same customer and product thread, with access and responsibility shaped by their role.

Customer and purpose

Identity, consent, communication preferences, need and product purpose remain attributable through the journey.

Policy and decision

Eligibility, pricing, documents, rules, human approvals and reasons follow the lender’s configured policy.

People and counterparties

Customer, staff, partner, supplier, dealer, assessor and service role context can travel with the case.

Evidence through closure

Key facts, documents, payment actions, servicing events, support, exceptions and closure remain traceable.
