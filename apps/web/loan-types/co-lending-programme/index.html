Where it fits

A journey for Participating institutions coordinating an eligible multi-lender programme.

LoanOS helps lending institutions shape the customer experience and the internal operating path around one governed record. The journey can be configured for the lender’s regulated entity, product policy, channel model and approved partners.

The Co-lending programme journey

Keep participant roles clear from allocation to servicing.

The journey gives each participating institution the context and evidence it needs without obscuring accountability.

  1. 01

    Define the arrangement

    Set participant roles, product scope, eligibility, allocation, servicing and settlement rules.

  2. 02

    Onboard the programme

    Record agreements, approvals, access, data boundaries, products and operational readiness.

  3. 03

    Assess and allocate the case

    Apply agreed policy, approval, eligibility and allocation logic with visible responsibility.

  4. 04

    Fund and reconcile

    Coordinate disbursement, participant allocation, settlement evidence and exception handling.

  5. 05

    Service with oversight

    Keep account servicing, statements, collections, reporting, audits and programme review connected.

What stays connected

The product context is not split across disconnected handoffs.

Teams work from the same customer and product thread, with access and responsibility shaped by their role.

Customer and purpose

Identity, consent, communication preferences, need and product purpose remain attributable through the journey.

Policy and decision

Eligibility, pricing, documents, rules, human approvals and reasons follow the lender’s configured policy.

People and counterparties

Customer, staff, partner, supplier, dealer, assessor and service role context can travel with the case.

Evidence through closure

Key facts, documents, payment actions, servicing events, support, exceptions and closure remain traceable.
