Where it fits

A journey for Institutions operating eligible domestic or cross-border trade programmes.

LoanOS helps lending institutions shape the customer experience and the internal operating path around one governed record. The journey can be configured for the lender’s regulated entity, product policy, channel model and approved partners.

The Trade finance workflow journey

Keep trade documents, approvals and release conditions in one flow.

The journey helps institutions orchestrate eligible trade programmes without splitting document control from credit control.

  1. 01

    Open the trade case

    Capture counterparty, trade purpose, programme, shipment or delivery details and required documents.

  2. 02

    Review documents and risk

    Coordinate product rules, counterparty checks, conditions, approvals and document completeness.

  3. 03

    Set release conditions

    Issue the approved facility terms, key facts, documentary requirements and authority trail.

  4. 04

    Control the transaction

    Track approved release, document movement, provider actions and settlement instructions.

  5. 05

    Reconcile and close

    Connect proceeds, repayments, exceptions, post-transaction evidence and account closure.

What stays connected

The product context is not split across disconnected handoffs.

Teams work from the same customer and product thread, with access and responsibility shaped by their role.

Customer and purpose

Identity, consent, communication preferences, need and product purpose remain attributable through the journey.

Policy and decision

Eligibility, pricing, documents, rules, human approvals and reasons follow the lender’s configured policy.

People and counterparties

Customer, staff, partner, supplier, dealer, assessor and service role context can travel with the case.

Evidence through closure

Key facts, documents, payment actions, servicing events, support, exceptions and closure remain traceable.
