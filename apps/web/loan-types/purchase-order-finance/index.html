The Purchase-order finance journey

Finance fulfilment against a credible order and delivery plan.

The journey keeps buyer, purchase order, supplier needs, milestones and expected payment connected.

  1. 01

    Capture the order

    Record the buyer, purchase order, supplier needs, delivery plan and expected payment.

  2. 02

    Validate fulfilment risk

    Review order credibility, buyer, margin, supplier, borrower capacity and policy conditions.

  3. 03

    Approve the cycle

    Set the draw, milestones, documents, pricing and controls for the short-cycle facility.

  4. 04

    Release against milestones

    Pay approved suppliers or borrower needs with purchase and delivery evidence.

  5. 05

    Collect the outcome

    Reconcile delivery, buyer payment, settlement allocation, exceptions and facility close.

What stays connected

The product context is not split across disconnected handoffs.

Teams work from the same customer and product thread, with access and responsibility shaped by their role.

Customer and purpose

Identity, consent, communication preferences, need and product purpose remain attributable through the journey.

Policy and decision

Eligibility, pricing, documents, rules, human approvals and reasons follow the lender’s configured policy.

People and counterparties

Customer, staff, partner, supplier, dealer, assessor and service role context can travel with the case.

Evidence through closure

Key facts, documents, payment actions, servicing events, support, exceptions and closure remain traceable.
