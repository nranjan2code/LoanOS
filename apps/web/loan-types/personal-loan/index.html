Where it fits

A journey for Retail customers with a defined eligible personal-purpose need.

LoanOS helps lending institutions shape the customer experience and the internal operating path around one governed record. The journey can be configured for the lender’s regulated entity, product policy, channel model and approved partners.

The Personal loan journey

Keep an unsecured retail journey clear and explainable.

The journey focuses on affordability, transparent terms, customer understanding and consistent servicing.

  1. 01

    Capture the need

    Record the customer request, purpose, channel, consent and required amount.

  2. 02

    Assess affordability

    Review identity, income, credit policy, repayment capacity and lender eligibility rules.

  3. 03

    Make the offer clear

    Present pricing, key facts, repayment schedule, documents and acceptance in plain language.

  4. 04

    Complete acceptance and payment

    Confirm conditions, agreement, verified beneficiary and controlled disbursement.

  5. 05

    Support to closure

    Keep repayments, statements, changes, support, collections and closure connected.

What stays connected

The product context is not split across disconnected handoffs.

Teams work from the same customer and product thread, with access and responsibility shaped by their role.

Customer and purpose

Identity, consent, communication preferences, need and product purpose remain attributable through the journey.

Policy and decision

Eligibility, pricing, documents, rules, human approvals and reasons follow the lender’s configured policy.

People and counterparties

Customer, staff, partner, supplier, dealer, assessor and service role context can travel with the case.

Evidence through closure

Key facts, documents, payment actions, servicing events, support, exceptions and closure remain traceable.
