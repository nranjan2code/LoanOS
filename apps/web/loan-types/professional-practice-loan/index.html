The Professional practice loan journey

Build around the practice, not just the borrower profile.

The journey connects professional income, practice plans and purposeful investment with a clear servicing model.

  1. 01

    Understand the practice plan

    Capture the profession, existing practice, investment purpose, location and expected income impact.

  2. 02

    Assess professional cash flow

    Review verified income, practice performance, affordability, product policy and approvals.

  3. 03

    Set clear terms

    Present the term, pricing, key facts, documents and conditions in a straightforward offer.

  4. 04

    Fund the practice need

    Control approved supplier, equipment, fit-out or borrower payment with supporting evidence.

  5. 05

    Support through maturity

    Keep schedule, account conduct, changes, support, collections and closure connected.

What stays connected

The product context is not split across disconnected handoffs.

Teams work from the same customer and product thread, with access and responsibility shaped by their role.

Customer and purpose

Identity, consent, communication preferences, need and product purpose remain attributable through the journey.

Policy and decision

Eligibility, pricing, documents, rules, human approvals and reasons follow the lender’s configured policy.

People and counterparties

Customer, staff, partner, supplier, dealer, assessor and service role context can travel with the case.

Evidence through closure

Key facts, documents, payment actions, servicing events, support, exceptions and closure remain traceable.
