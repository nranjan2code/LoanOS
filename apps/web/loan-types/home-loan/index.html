The Home loan journey

Bring the customer, property and disbursement plan together.

A home loan journey needs income, property, legal, technical and payment milestones to stay connected.

  1. 01

    Plan the purchase

    Capture borrowers, property, own contribution, seller or developer and required loan amount.

  2. 02

    Assess customer and property

    Review income, affordability, credit policy, legal checks, technical review and valuation.

  3. 03

    Sanction with conditions

    Set terms, key facts, documents, property conditions and the approved disbursement plan.

  4. 04

    Disburse by milestone

    Control pre-disbursement conditions, seller or developer payment, stage evidence and security creation.

  5. 05

    Serve to release

    Manage schedule, statements, changes, support, collections, closure and security satisfaction.

What stays connected

The product context is not split across disconnected handoffs.

Teams work from the same customer and product thread, with access and responsibility shaped by their role.

Customer and purpose

Identity, consent, communication preferences, need and product purpose remain attributable through the journey.

Policy and decision

Eligibility, pricing, documents, rules, human approvals and reasons follow the lender’s configured policy.

People and counterparties

Customer, staff, partner, supplier, dealer, assessor and service role context can travel with the case.

Evidence through closure

Key facts, documents, payment actions, servicing events, support, exceptions and closure remain traceable.
