What stays consistent

Every product still needs a complete operating story.

Product differences matter. The responsibilities beneath them remain connected.

01

Product readiness

Regulated entity, borrower class, amount, tenor, pricing, fees, charges, security and channels are explicit.

02

Customer and consent

Identity, economic profile, beneficial ownership, notices, purpose and permission stay attributable.

03

Decision and explanation

Rules, models, data, policy version, reasons, reviewers and approvals remain reconstructable.

04

Documents and fund flows

KFS, sanction, execution, beneficiary, disbursement and payment rails follow product-specific controls.

05

Servicing through closure

Schedule, ledger, statements, changes, collections, hardship, closure and security satisfaction stay in scope.
