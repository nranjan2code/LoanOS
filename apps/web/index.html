Where lending work breaks

The handoff is usually the problem.

Credit, operations, servicing and compliance should not have to reconstruct the same loan in separate systems.

01 / BEFORE

Credit approves with conditions.

Operations receives the same case, documents, policy result and approval conditions—without rebuilding the file.

02 / DURING

An exception needs judgement.

The accountable person sees what changed, why the rule referred it and which evidence supports the decision.

03 / AFTER

The loan enters servicing.

Sanctioned terms, communications and repayment context continue into the account instead of disappearing at booking.

04 / ALWAYS

Someone asks, “why?”

The actor, policy version, consent, document and reason are already attached to the action—not reconstructed later.
