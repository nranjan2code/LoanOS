Compliance Controls Core

LoanOS India builds regulatory mandates directly into the transactional layer rather than depending on post-facto audit reports.

🏢
Regulated Entity Trust
Ensures every transaction belongs to an active, licensed RBI RE with grievance officer details and board policies.
🚫
LSP Fund-Flow Isolation
Hard-blocks lending service provider pass-through accounts. Enforces direct bank-to-borrower fund routing.
📄
KFS & Charge Ceiling
Locks charges to pre-disclosed limits, blocks undiagnosed penal interest compounding, and validates floating interest resets.
🔏
DPDP Retention & Rights
Gates data erasure against the 5-year PMLA/RBI retention clock. Restricts sharing to active purpose-based consent logs.
🤖
AI Model Risk Safeguards
Monitors drift, forces validation signoffs for credit models, and houses automated model and global kill switches.
⛓️
Verifiable Audit Spine
Attests transaction truth by sealing every event into a cryptographic, SHA-256 chain mapped directly to the RE.
