Straight answers

The questions serious buyers ask.

We already have an LOS and LMS. Why LoanOS?

Those systems originate and service. LoanOS makes compliance an enforced part of that flow and preserves why across the handoffs—one evidence spine, replayable decisions and an exportable record.

Are you RBI certified or guaranteed compliant?

No. Certification depends on your legal, security and integration review. We show which controls are enforced in software and label what is built, deployment-specific or on the roadmap.

How do you address multi-tenant and exit risk?

Isolation is exercised through cross-tenant regression tests, with Postgres RLS and dedicated deployment boundaries available where required. Portability includes a reproducible, re-loadable tenant export.

Can we evaluate without production data?

Yes. The evaluation path uses synthetic borrowers and mock integration boundaries before any live-data or production discussion.
