Connect the ecosystem

An integration is part of the control story.

LoanOS keeps the external action, provider, purpose, masked identifiers, result and downstream decision traceable.

Identity, KYC and beneficial ownership

Connector boundaries for CKYC, V-CIP and verification evidence can support the lender’s approved process while prohibited Aadhaar artefacts remain out of storage.

Bureau, bank-account and cash-flow data

External results are tied to the application, consent, provider, time and decision context rather than copied into an unexplained score.

Payments and mandates

NACH, UPI and bank-account verification evidence supports direct, attributable disbursement and repayment workflows.

eSign, documents and communications

KFS, sanction, agreement, privacy, statement and notice delivery can carry checksums, delivery receipts and masked-recipient evidence.

Models and decision services

Model calls are allowed only through inventory, status, validation, purpose, human-review and kill-switch gates.
