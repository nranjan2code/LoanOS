# Missing-Capability Closure Wave

This wave moves 39 catalogue capabilities from Missing to Partial through four cohesive, fail-closed control modules.

| Bundle | Executable scope | Production depth still required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Platform and product | Tenant commercial plans, module/limit authorization, sequential environment promotion, portability reload reconciliation, tenant operational communications, risk-pricing matrices, product version diff/impact, optional add-ons and exact profitability/RAROC parameters. | Billing/CRM/deployment adapters, authoring UI, actuarial and finance calibration, production promotion authority. |
| KYC and underwriting | Guardian/special-category authority, advance/post-due KYC actions, low-risk grace monitoring, BC-assisted acknowledgement, physical-original custody, bank cash-flow/bounce/stability analysis, source verification, household FOIR/MFI indebtedness and deterministic risk grade/price. | Live source feeds, institution policies/models, CKYCRR/BC operations, physical vault integration and independent validation. |
| Collateral and disbursement | Independent panels/orders, valuation/legal review, exact LTV/haircut/eligible value, insurance/perfection/custody, inspection/covenants/EWS/revaluation, closure release, checksum-bound multiparty signing, certified tranches, supplier invoices/end use and follow-up. | Live registry/insurer/valuer/counsel/custodian/payment adapters and institution-secured-product operations. |
| Servicing and collections | Due-date/EMI/tenure/mode requests, ledger-bound certificates, deterministic strategies/treatments, agency geography/capacity allocation, exact fee/tax settlement and roll/cure/liquidation analytics. Existing succession and language controls also evidence representative/deceased servicing and preferred-language delivery. | Borrower/service UI, schedule mutation adapters, live agencies/dialers, finance posting, workforce feeds and production analytics history. |

Money entering these controls is represented as integer-paise strings; ratios are bounded basis points or exact decimal strings. Material configuration, financial, custody, release and allocation actions require independent approval.
