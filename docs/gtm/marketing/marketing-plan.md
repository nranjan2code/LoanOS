# Marketing Plan

Demand strategy for a compliance-led, regulated-buyer B2B product. Small, targeted
market (India RBI-regulated lenders) → **account-based, credibility-first**, not
broad-funnel spray.

## Objectives (set targets with leadership)

1. **Credibility** — be the reference for "provable digital-lending compliance" in
   India lending circles.
2. **Pipeline** — sourced + influenced qualified opportunities into the sales
   motion (`../sales/sales-playbook.md`).
3. **Sales enablement** — keep every claim proof-backed and every rep equipped.

## Strategy: ABM + thought leadership

The buyer universe is countable (banks, SFBs, NBFCs, HFCs, co-ops, AI-FIs). Favour
depth over reach:

- **Tier-1 named accounts:** 1:1 ABM — custom evidence-pack demos, exec briefings.
- **Tier-2:** 1:few by segment (NBFC / SFB / HFC) — segment campaigns.
- **Tier-3 / long tail:** 1:many nurture — content, webinars, newsletter.

## Channels (ranked by fit)

| Channel | Why it fits | Play |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Founder/exec thought leadership | Trust is the currency; regulated buyers follow credible voices | LinkedIn POV posts on DLD 2025, model risk, evidence-by-design |
| Regulatory-moment content | Buyers act on RBI changes | Rapid explainers tied to each direction/draft; "what it means for enforcement" |
| Webinars / roundtables | Peer proof + education | CCO/CRO roundtables on model-risk readiness, DLA compliance |
| Analyst / industry bodies | Third-party credibility | Briefings; fintech/NBFC association engagement |
| Targeted outbound (ABM) | Small named universe | Coordinated with sales sequences (`../sales/email-sequences.md`) |
| Case studies / references | De-risks the buy | Publish as design partners convert (template in `../assets/case-study-template.md`) |
| SEO / owned content | Capture active research | Pillar pages on KFS enforcement, AI kill switch, DLG, co-lending |

**Deprioritise:** broad paid social, generic PPC, mass email. The market is too
narrow and the buyer too skeptical for spray-and-pray.

## Funnel & content map

| Stage | Buyer question | Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Awareness | "Am I exposed on digital-lending compliance?" | POV posts, regulatory explainers, "audit fire drill" narrative |
| Consideration | "Can compliance be enforced, not documented?" | Pillar pages, webinars, product explainer, one-pager |
| Evaluation | "Will it pass my InfoSec + prove ROI?" | Vendor-posture pack, security FAQ, sandbox, evidence-pack demo, ROI framing |
| Decision | "Can I trust them and leave if needed?" | Case studies, exit/portability proof, references |
| Expansion | "What else can we enforce?" | Module content (AI Governance, Distribution, Integrations) |

## Signature narratives (own these)

1. **"Audit is an export, not a fire drill."** (Pillar 1)
2. **"The AI kill switch that's actually wired in."** (Pillar 3)
3. **"Compliance-as-code, not compliance-as-content."** (category)
4. **"Exit is a feature."** (foundation / procurement)

## Design-partner / reference program

Early on, credibility > volume. Recruit a small number of design-partner REs
across segments; trade deep collaboration + preferential terms for references and
case studies. This seeds Tier-1 ABM proof.

## Measurement

- Leading: content engagement by named accounts, meeting acceptance rate,
  webinar attendance by ICP title.
- Pipeline: sourced/influenced qualified opps (Stage-2+), win rate by segment,
  security-review pass rate.
- Health: % of live assets passing `gtm-backlog-sync.mjs` (claims discipline).

## Compliance-of-marketing guardrail

Every asset ships only after `node docs/gtm/automation/gtm-backlog-sync.mjs`
passes and a human confirms no `Partial`/`Roadmap` claim is stated as live. In
this market, one overclaim costs more than ten campaigns earn.
