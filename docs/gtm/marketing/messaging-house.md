# Messaging House

The canonical hierarchy every asset inherits from. If a headline, ad, or deck
slide isn't traceable to a pillar and a `Built` proof point, rewrite it.

## Roof — brand promise (one line)

**Compliance you can prove, produced while the loan runs.**

## Positioning line (longer)

The India-only lending operating system where every KFS disclosure, consent,
fund-flow rule, and AI-model decision is a machine-enforced control with a
tamper-evident, byte-replayable audit trail — origination, servicing, workflow,
and model governance in one system.

## Three pillars (walls)

### Pillar 1 — Evidence produced in-flow
*Message:* Stop reconstructing compliance after the fact. Controls are enforced
gates, and evidence is a by-product of the flow.
*Proofs:* KFS-before-contract (C-02), consent/DPDP ledger (C-24/26), fund-flow
guard (C-03), India-only + Aadhaar-prohibition (C-01/04), audit hash chain +
export (C-05/06).
*Headline options:* "Audit is an export, not a fire drill." / "KFS as a control,
not a PDF."

### Pillar 2 — Decisions you can trust and replay
*Message:* A per-tenant, fail-closed decision engine runs your credit policy as
signed, versioned, replayable rules.
*Proofs:* per-tenant engine (C-14), signed policy bundles (C-15), exact-decimal
math (C-16), byte-replay (C-17).
*Headline options:* "Replay any decision, byte-for-byte." / "Your policy, signed
and versioned."

### Pillar 3 — AI under human command
*Message:* Model governance wired into the runtime decision path, with a kill
switch you can actually pull.
*Proofs:* kill switch (C-09), independent-validation gate (C-10), drift auto-trip
(C-11), generative red-team gate (C-12), post-incident-review-to-clear (C-13).
*Headline options:* "Stop any model. Instantly. Fail-closed." / "The kill switch
that's actually wired in."

## Supporting proof band (foundation)

Multi-tenant isolation proven in CI (C-07), Postgres RLS (C-08), disclosed
sub-processor register (C-29), 6-hour incident clocks (C-30), audited break-glass
(C-31), re-loadable exit export (C-32/33). *Message:* "Built to satisfy your RE
outsourcing obligations — and to let you leave."

## Proof-first writing rule

Every claim in market = a `Built` proof point (see
`../strategy/claims-and-backlog-sync.md`). `Partial` → "first slice / governed
boundary." `Roadmap` → "on our roadmap." No exceptions, no adjectives standing in
for evidence.

## Brand alignment

Verbal voice here is the counterpart to the visual brand in
[`../brand/brand-guide.md`](../brand/brand-guide.md) — precise, calm, evidence-led,
honest about maturity. The site's own line, *"Accountable by construction,"* is the
brand-level expression of this messaging house. Apply the brand's colours and type
to any rendered asset.

## Words we use / avoid

**Use:** enforce, gate, evidence, replay, fail-closed, tamper-evident, in-flow,
governed boundary, India-hosted, RBI Digital Lending Directions 2025, KFS, kill
switch.

**Avoid:** "RBI certified," "guaranteed compliant," "fully automated compliance,"
"AI-powered" as a standalone boast, any live-integration claim that is `Partial`.
