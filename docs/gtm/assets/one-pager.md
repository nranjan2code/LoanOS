## LoanOS India
**Compliance you can prove, produced while the loan runs.**

The India-only lending operating system for RBI-regulated lenders — banks, SFBs,
NBFCs, HFCs, co-operative banks, and AI-FIs. Origination, servicing, workflow, and
AI-model governance in one auditable platform.

### The problem
India lending platforms break at the handoffs. Origination can't tell servicing
*why* a loan was approved. KFS and disclosures become documents, not controls.
LSPs are integrated but the regulated entity carries the liability. AI influences
credit while model governance sits in separate paperwork. Compliance evidence is
assembled after the fact — slow, risky, incomplete.

### What LoanOS does differently
Every KFS disclosure, consent, fund-flow rule, and model decision is a
machine-enforced control with a tamper-evident, byte-replayable audit trail.

**Evidence in-flow** — KFS must be disclosed before any contract executes;
disbursement can't route through an LSP pass-through account; Aadhaar artefacts are
never stored; every state change is sealed into a per-tenant audit hash chain you
can export as an integrity-attested pack.

**Decisions you can replay** — a per-tenant, fail-closed decision engine runs your
credit policy as content-hashed, four-eyes-approved, signed bundles with
exact-decimal math; any decision replays byte-for-byte from its audit record.

**AI under human command** — model inventory with an independent-validation gate,
a model/global kill switch wired into the runtime path, drift that auto-trips the
switch, and post-incident review required to clear it.

**Built for your outsourcing obligations** — hard multi-tenant isolation proven in
CI, optional Postgres RLS, disclosed sub-processor register, 6-hour CERT-In/RBI
incident clocks, audited break-glass, and a re-loadable exit export. India-hosted.

### Where we're honest
We don't claim certification — that's your legal, security, and integration review.
Live certified integrations (credit bureaus, FINnet, CKYC, live payment rails) and
SOC 2 / ISO 27001 are on our roadmap; governed submission boundaries exist today.
