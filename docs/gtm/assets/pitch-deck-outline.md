## Narrative rules

- Slide 12 (built vs. roadmap) is **mandatory** — it earns trust and pre-empts the
  "is this real?" objection. Never hide it.
- One idea per slide. The line in the table is the only sentence that must be said.
- The demo moment (slide 10) is the emotional peak — protect time for it.

## Variants

- **Compliance-buyer variant:** expand slides 7 & 12; lead with evidence.
- **Risk-buyer variant:** expand slides 8 & 9; lead with the engine + kill switch.
- **Engineering/security variant:** expand slide 11; open with isolation + exit.
- **Exec/short variant (6 slides):** 1, 2, 6, 7–9 merged, 10, 14.
